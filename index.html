Scan / Photograph Label

Use the iPad's built-in Scan Text / Live Text feature to put the label text directly into the box below. You can also photograph the label for reference.

On iPad: tap inside this box to bring up the keyboard, then tap the camera icon / Scan Text option (the exact button varies by iPadOS version). Point the camera at the label and insert the detected text. This uses Apple's built-in Live Text recognition and does not require internet access or an OCR library.
If your label has a barcode, the app will also try the browser's built-in barcode detector when available. Barcode support varies by iPadOS/Safari version.
